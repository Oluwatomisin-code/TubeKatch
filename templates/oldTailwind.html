About TubeKatch?

TubeKatch is an online video downloader developed by Tomi. It's an excellent service that helps you download YouTube videos free of charge. It also allows you to trim sections of a video for your needs.

How to use?

  1. Copy the YouTube video URL and paste it into the input field above. Then click the "Fetch Video" button.
  2. Choose the video format from the dropdown menu.
  3. Click the "Download Now" button to download the full video.
  4. If you want to trim the video, check the "Trim?" checkbox.
  5. Use the slider to select the start and end times of the video section you want to trim.
  6. Click the "Download Now" button to download the trimmed video.
